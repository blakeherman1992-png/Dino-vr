A gaze-controlled walk · 68 million BC

CretaceousField Trip

Five animals are out in the valley. Walk the trail, hold your gaze on one, and it goes in your field journal. No buttons, no controller — your head does everything.

  • LOOKGlowing rings on the ground are trail stops. Rest the dot on one and you glide over.
  • HOLDRest the dot on an animal for about a second to log it. The card tells you what you found.
  • FINDAll five are loggable from the trail. Some of them notice you first.

Best in Safari on iPhone, held sideways. If head tracking doesn't take, drag a finger to look around and tap Recenter once the phone is in the headset.
